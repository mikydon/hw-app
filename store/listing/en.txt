HW App: Home Workout
Home workouts in 19 min, your own plans, ranks, streaks and friends.
💪 HW App is a workout plan that grows with you. Train at home with your own body weight and no equipment, or build your own plans with dumbbells and gym machines.

📋 <b>The plan</b>
• Recommended: three workout days (A, B, C), every other day. Each one trains push, pull, legs and core in ~19 min, ~13 min, or Only 5 minutes on a busy day.
• My plans: build your own from 134 exercises (with or without equipment), set them to weekdays and sort them into groups.
• Library (with an account): find plans from other people, try one before you save it, and give stars to plans you trained.
• The app shows what you did last time and a +1 goal, for reps and for weights, so you keep progressing.

⏱️ <b>During the workout</b>
• A rest timer with +15, +30 and +60 s that keeps counting on the lock screen, with a beep and vibration.
• Notes, extra exercises, a tempo guide and a how-to for every exercise.
• A warm-up before and an optional cool-down after.

🏆 <b>Progress and motivation</b>
• 9 ranks for every exercise, from Iron to Obsidian, and a body map that ranks 9 body parts.
• 50 badges, XP and levels, a streak with freeze days, weekly challenges and an evening reminder before your streak ends.
• A calendar and progress charts, plus a weekly recap and your year in numbers as pictures to share.
• Diamonds you earn by training, for streak freezes, XP boosts, profile looks and card styles.
• 🃏 Muscle Deck: a card game where your training is your strength. Ranked: play the same deal as a real player and climb from Iron to Obsidian.

🍎 <b>Calories</b>
• A calorie calculator with goals to lose, keep or gain weight, and a daily log with a calendar.
• Optional Health Connect: steps, food from other apps, weight and height. This data never leaves your phone.

👥 <b>Friends (optional, 16+)</b>
• Sign in with Google or e-mail, then add friends by code, link or nickname.
• Together: keep a workout or calorie streak with a friend, set a weekly goal and nudge each other.
• Profiles, a friends leaderboard, messages, notifications, a cloud backup and sync between your devices.

✨ <b>Also</b>
• 15 languages, a light, dark or automatic theme, 6 main colours and a bigger text size.
• No ads and no tracking. Without an account, everything stays on your phone.

HW App is made by one developer. Ideas and bug reports are welcome on GitHub: github.com/mikydon/hw-app
